Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions regarding how ResearchMind AI processes PDFs, manages API keys, or handles grounded citations?

What is ResearchMind AI?

ResearchMind AI is an agentic academic research intelligence platform. It ingests scientific publications from arXiv or local PDF uploads, parses mathematical structures, generates in-depth 6-pillar scientific analyses, and supports conversational Q&A with grounded page citations.

How does ResearchMind ground its answers?

During PDF parsing, ResearchMind demarcates exact page markers and section headings across the document. Prompts enforce strict citations (e.g. `[Page 3, Section 4.1]`) and mathematical veracity in KaTeX LaTeX notation.

Which Gemini models are supported?

ResearchMind supports Google's active Gemini 3.x foundational models: Gemini 3.5 Flash (Recommended), Gemini 3.5 Flash Lite, Gemini 3.6 Flash, and Gemini 3.8 Flash, backed by an automatic resilient model fallback system.

Can I compare multiple research papers simultaneously?

Yes! Toggle the workspace to "Compare" mode, select 2 or more publications from your paper library, and click "Synthesize Comparison" to generate an objective structured cross-paper matrix.

Where is my data and research stored?

All parsed PDFs and analysis reports are persisted locally in your project's SQLite database (`data/researchmind.db`) and local filesystem. Your research remains private on your machine.
